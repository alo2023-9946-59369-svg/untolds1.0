HOW TO PLAY
  1. Select a deck by clicking the dots below or tapping the stacked cards
  2. Click START GAME to begin
  3. Click DRAW NEW CARD — the card will automatically flip to reveal the question
  4. Answer according to the rule at the bottom of the card
  5. Click DRAW NEW CARD again for the next question
  6. Use the settings menu to toggle dark mode or sound effects
ABOUT UNTOLDS

UNTOLDS is a digital conversation card game designed to help people build deeper connections through meaningful conversations.

With multiple decks tailored for different relationships and situations, UNTOLDS provides a safe space for sharing thoughts, feelings, and stories that often go unspoken.

Perfect for friends, couples, families, or even as a personal reflection tool. Start conversations that matter.
